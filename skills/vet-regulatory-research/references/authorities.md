before relying on any entry. Regulators are reorganized, renamed and merged, and different
product categories are often regulated by different bodies in the same country.

## International Harmonization Bodies

Check whether the target country has adopted any of these — it directly affects dossier format
requirements and whether reference approvals from other jurisdictions are accepted (Section 3).

| Body | Relevance |
|------|-----------|
| **VICH** | EU, USA, Canada, Japan, Australia, NZ — harmonized technical requirements; check if target country accepts VICH-based dossiers |
| **WOAH / OIE** | Global animal health standards; disease classification affects vaccine registration; some countries adopt WOAH standards |
| **Codex Alimentarius** | MRL standards for food-producing animals adopted by many countries; key for withdrawal period and residue compliance |
| **ASEAN Harmonization** | ASEAN member states shared guidelines for vet products |
| **AU-NZ TTMRA** | Australia / New Zealand mutual recognition arrangement |
| **SADC** | Southern African Development Community harmonization efforts |
| **GCC Veterinary Products Law** | GCC member states shared framework for vet product registration; relevant for UAE, Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Qatar, Bahrain, Oman |
| **PIC/S** | Membership may allow GMP inspection waivers based on PIC/S-member inspection reports |
