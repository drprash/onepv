# Claude Code Skills

A collection of Claude Code skills for pharmacovigilance, regulatory affairs, and clinical research.

## Skills

### [clinical-2x2-study-analyzer](clinical-2x2-study-analyzer/SKILL.md)
Extracts binary outcome data from raw clinical study text and produces a full epidemiological analysis. Builds a 2×2 contingency table, calculates relative risk, odds ratio, absolute risk reduction, NNT/NNH, 95% confidence intervals, and runs chi-square or Fisher exact tests. Outputs publication-ready tables and manuscript-ready summaries.

### [pharma-regulatory-research](pharma-regulatory-research/SKILL.md)
Researches pharmaceutical product registration requirements for any country and produces a structured, business-plan-ready report. Covers registration pathways (standard, abridged, reliance, fast track, biosimilar, generic), step-by-step procedures, fees in local currency and USD, timelines, and business considerations. Tailored to the applicant's role (local manufacturer, foreign MAH, local agent, distributor, etc.).

### [pv-regulatory-research](pv-regulatory-research/SKILL.md)
Researches pharmacovigilance regulatory obligations for any country. Covers QPPV/local PV contact requirements, PSMF, expedited ICSR reporting (7-day/15-day), PSUR/PBRER aggregate reports, signal management, risk management plans, literature monitoring, and reliance on international documents. Produces a consolidated PV obligations summary table and an optional compliance readiness checklist. Includes built-in reference tables for GCC, EU, US, LATAM, and Africa.

### [serialization-regulatory-research](serialization-regulatory-research/SKILL.md)
Researches serialization and track & trace (T&T) requirements for any product category in any country. Covers unique identifier structure, barcode/label technical specifications, aggregation, supply chain obligations by role (MAH, manufacturer, importer, wholesaler, dispenser), national repository registration, anti-counterfeiting features, implementation timelines, fees, and penalties. Includes reference tables for EU FMD/EMVS, US DSCSA, EAEU MDLP, GCC (Tatmeen/Salama), LATAM, Africa, and India.

### [vet-regulatory-research](vet-regulatory-research/SKILL.md)
Researches veterinary product registration requirements for any country across all product categories — veterinary medicines, vaccines and biologics, medicated feed, feed additives, nutritional supplements, diagnostics, disinfectants, and pet food. Accounts for multi-authority structures (vet medicines and feed additives are often regulated separately), species-specific requirements (MRL/withdrawal periods for food-producing animals vs. companion animals), and vaccine batch release obligations. Tailored to the applicant's role and target species.
